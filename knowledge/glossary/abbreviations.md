| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| LMV | Live Master View: the live grid; its daily archive is `lmv_daily` |
| EMV | Inception "View by Date": historic end-of-day view and EOD reports (computed in the app) |
| HMV | Historical Master View: the Inception grid over a date range (computed in the app) |
| OHLC / OI | Open-High-Low-Close / open interest |
| ATP | Average traded price (`avg_rate`) |
| UF / FD | Unfilled / filled (gap areas) |
| `_I` / `_II` | Near-month / next-month continuous futures series (`RELIANCE_I`) |
